TrustLens Technical Architecture & Specifications

1. Biometric Engine

  • InsightFace (buffalo_l): SCRFD landmark face detection + ArcFace 512-d feature extraction.
  • Normalized Embeddings: Float32 L2-normalized vectors with deterministic SHA-256 byte hashing.
  • Multi-Face & Group Handling: Explicit face selection on queries and exhaustive group evaluation on candidate images.
  • Quality Gate: Composite metrics (sharpness, exposure, resolution, landmark symmetry).

2. Decentralized Discovery & Storage

  • SerpApi Google Lens: Multi-source discovery across supported social platforms and the indexed web.
  • IPFS via Pinata: Content-addressed immutable storage with multi-gateway resolution fallback.
  • RFC-8785 Canonical JSON: Deterministic key sorting and compact UTF-8 byte serialization.

3. Blockchain Proof Registry

  • Polygon Amoy (Chain ID 80002): EVM smart contract ProofRegistry.sol at 0x6D03eE0515FeeA663D6fe79F8e12dDA4C24B3c5F.
  • Anti-Overwrite Protection: Reverts duplicate registrations to prevent unauthorized rewrites.
  • Minimal Footprint: Only cryptographic fingerprints and CIDs are anchored on-chain.
